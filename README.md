## Executive Overview

**Code Circle | دائرة برمجية** develops practical, customizable, and scalable software and digital solutions around real business needs.

This repository presents the **Software Solutions** category of the Code Circle portfolio: a family of sector-focused business applications designed to support specialized operational and administrative needs across defined business domains.

The software portfolio currently presented here includes:

- **Farha | فرحة** — Event hall and booking management
- **Irada | إيراد** — Financial operations management
- **Tazamun | تزامن** — Installment and collection management
- **Watad | وتد** — Property and unit management
- **Naseej | نسيج** — Human resources management

These products are presented publicly to communicate their professional scope, business purpose, and the software-development capability represented by their design and implementation. Source code, architecture, database design, internal workflows, detailed business rules, and proprietary implementation methods remain private.

## Software Portfolio

### 🎉 Farha | فرحة

**Event Hall & Booking Management Solution**

Farha is a sector-focused business application designed for managing event halls and related operational activity.

At a public level, its business scope covers:

- Event hall management
- Bookings
- Contracts
- Services
- Payments
- Operational reporting

The product is intended to bring these recurring business activities into a more organized digital environment while supporting clearer operational follow-up and management visibility.

The solution represents Code Circle's approach to turning recurring financial administration into a more structured, reviewable, and professionally managed digital process.
